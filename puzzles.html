Lesson 8

Tactics puzzles

Fifteen positions from real patterns. Click the piece you want to move, then click where it should go. Wrong answers just shake the board, so try as many times as you like.

0 puzzles solved

How to solve a puzzle. Look at every check first, then every capture, then every threat. The answer is almost always one of those, because a forcing move takes your opponent's choices away.

Checkmate in one

Find the single move that ends the game. Take your time and look at every check.

Win material

Nobody gets checkmated here. Win a piece, and win the game later.

Checkmate in two

Your move, their answer, then mate. You have to see two moves ahead.
