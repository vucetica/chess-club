Try it

This is the starting position of every chess game. Click any piece, white or black, and its legal moves light up: a green dot is an empty square, a red ring is a capture.

Club tip. Some useful tips will be in the boxes like this one.

How to use this site

  • Click on the boards. Click a piece to see its legal moves, then click a square to play one.
  • Walkthroughs. Press Next to play through an opening or a checkmate one move at a time. Press Back to take back a move.
  • Puzzles. Play the move you think is best. If it is wrong, the board shakes and you try again.
