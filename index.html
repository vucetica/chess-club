Try it

This is how every chess game starts. Click any piece, white or black, and every square it is allowed to move to lights up. A green dot means an empty square it can move to. A red ring means a piece it can capture.

Club tip. Handy tips show up in boxes like this one, so keep an eye out for them.

How to use this site

  • Click on the boards. Click a piece to see its legal moves, then click a square to play one.
  • Move by move. Press Next to play through an opening or a checkmate one move at a time, and press Back if you want to see a move again.
  • Puzzles. Play the move you think is best. If it is wrong, the board shakes and you try again.
