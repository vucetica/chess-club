Lesson 8

Tactics puzzles

Click the piece you want to move, then click where it should go. Wrong answers just shake the board, so try as many times as you like.

Did you know? Chess puzzles are more than a thousand years old. Arabic players collected them in books called mansubat in the 800s and 900s, and some of those puzzles still work on a modern board, even though a few of the pieces moved differently back then.

0 puzzles solved

How to solve a puzzle. Look at every check first, then every capture, then every threat. The answer is almost always one of those three, because a move your opponent is forced to answer takes all their other choices away.

Checkmate in one

Find the single move that ends the game. Take your time and look at every check.

Win material

Nobody gets checkmated here. Your goal is to win a piece.

Checkmate in two

Your move, their answer, then mate. You have to see two moves ahead.

Where to go next? Play chess! Every move you make in a real game is a little puzzle of its own.
